Privacy Policy

DOT — Day Of Timeline  |  Effective February 27, 2026

Overview

DOT ("the App") is a wedding day timeline app built by CclutePhotography ("we", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple ID identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address. We use this to create and manage your account.

Timeline data: Timeline names, event titles, times, notes, shot lists, venue names, and wedding dates that you enter into the App. This data is stored to provide the App's core functionality.

Team membership: When you join or share a timeline, we store your role (e.g., photographer, coordinator) and associate you with that timeline so all team members can collaborate.

Device tokens: We collect push notification tokens (APNs and Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send you event reminders and timeline updates. We also collect Live Activity push tokens to keep your Lock Screen widget in sync.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect photos, contacts, location data, or health data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and sync your wedding day timelines across devices and team members.
  • To send push notifications for event reminders and timeline updates.
  • To display Live Activities on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
  • To manage your DOT Pro subscription status.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database hosting, user authentication, and real-time data sync. Data is stored on Supabase's servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google) — Push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging and analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple for authentication and StoreKit for subscription management. Apple Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Your account and timeline data are retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be removed from our systems. Timelines you created may be retained for other team members unless all members leave.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. You can also manage notification permissions in your device's Settings app.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by updating the effective date above.
